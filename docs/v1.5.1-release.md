# v1.5.1 — Kitchen Architecture Expression

## Summary

A targeted refinement of v1.5.0 makes Runs, Rails, Surfaces and Plans perceptible
through a handful of composed moments. Seven frontend files change. The approved
opening, photography, shared styles, motion source, project data and gallery
algorithm are retained. No dependencies, assets, client scripts or animation
libraries were added.

## Stage 1 — Two specific corrections

### Process hierarchy

Home and Process retain their five stages, ordered lists, headings, descriptions,
photographs and sequence. The separate 26px stage-number paragraphs become 14px
references beside the narrative labels. Titles now lead directly into the
photographs and copy. “Step by step” and “Five clear stages” become a journey-led
introduction; the homepage summary emphasises guidance rather than counting.
The Services process bridge also drops its separate numbers, retaining its
three-item ordered sequence and link to the complete process.

The first-stage production build and browser review confirmed all five stages,
14px references and 43.2px desktop stage headings before the expression work.

### Connected composition

The homepage process ending previously used `justify-content: space-between`
across the full container, separating its statement from its link. They now
share one bounded composition with a local gap. The projects browse action sits
directly beneath its introduction instead of floating after the featured
project. Closing reassurance and actions share an inset surface.

No screenshot attachment was present in the request. The process ending matched
the described heading/statement versus distant-action problem and was checked
explicitly against the baseline. Section spacing was not tightened globally.

Specification rows respond to the width of their own panel. Narrow panels stack
label and value; wider panels use two columns. This prevents “Completed” from
breaking mid-word and keeps enlarged labels readable without fixed heights.
